## Finalized product hypothesis

Based on Aisha’s qualitative signal that discovery feels like a chore her segment opts out of, and the Snapshot 3 finding that Wanderers churn 22 points lower when exposed to Spotlight (the largest effect of any segment, across 41% of the base), I believe that removing the effort of discovery for Wanderers — surfacing curated content they’ll actually finish instead of a passive trending feed — will reduce their churn and flatten the Month 0→1 retention leak, as measured by a lift in curated-engagement rate among exposed Wanderers within 6 to 8 weeks and a sustained double-digit churn improvement over a matched control. I will protect Power User retention and session length as the guardrail, and I will make a go/no-go decision to scale once the Spotlight cohorts reach Month 5 to 6 and confirm the flatter retention curve holds.

## Success metrics

| Metric | Type | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|

| Weekly Wanderer sessions | Leading indicator | 1.1 → 1.6+ per week vs control (6–8 wks) | Earliest signal discovery stopped being a chore; your tripwire |
| Power User retention + session length | Guardrail | No drop >1 pt; stays 58%+ curated | Protects the valuable 22% you already retain from curation dilution |
